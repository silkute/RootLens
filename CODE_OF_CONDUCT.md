# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique code and decisions, not people.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, disclosure of private information and
deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation. Report
conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through their published
contact channel; do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Maintainers should
acknowledge reports, protect reporter privacy and explain proportionate actions.
